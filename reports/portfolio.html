01

Business problem

A blueberry pack-size change and a missing juice conversion left the September stock figures incomplete.

This case checks the receipts for one warehouse and eight SKUs, corrects the quantities supported by supplier specifications, and compares them with physical stock counts. The review date is 2 October 2026, ahead of production on 3 October.
